# 🏛️ Civic Praxis

### AI-Powered Citizen Grievance & Government Order Auto-Routing System

Civic Praxis is an AI-powered Smart Governance platform designed to simplify and improve the management of citizen grievances and government orders.

The system uses **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** to analyze complaints, identify multiple issues, determine the appropriate government departments, detect priority levels, and automatically route issues for faster resolution.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

In traditional grievance management systems, citizen complaints are often manually read and forwarded to the appropriate government departments.

This process can be:

* ⏳ Time-consuming
* ❌ Prone to human errors
* 🔄 Difficult to manage when a complaint contains multiple issues
* 📉 Inefficient for large numbers of complaints

For example:

> "The road is damaged, the streetlight is not working, and the drainage is blocked."

This single complaint contains multiple issues that belong to different departments.

---

## 💡 Solution

Civic Praxis automatically analyzes the complaint using AI and separates it into multiple issues.

### Example:

| Issue                  | Department              |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| 🛣️ Road Damage        | Public Works Department |
| 💡 Streetlight Failure | Electricity Department  |
| 🚰 Drainage Blockage   | Sanitation Department   |

Each issue can then be independently routed to the responsible department.
